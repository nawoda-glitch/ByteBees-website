Literature Survey

Research Gap 01

Current Smart Apiculture

Existing research in **smart apiculture** primarily focuses on **environmental monitoring** using temperature, humidity, and sound sensors. While these systems provide real-time data, they often lack **predictive intelligence** and **integrated decision-support mechanisms**.

Missing: Scalability
Research Gap 02

Machine Learning Models

Models such as **LSTM** and **Random Forest** have shown promise in forecasting hive parameters. However, their implementations remain **experimental**, with limited **deployment** in **resource-constrained rural settings**.

Missing: Interpretability
Research Gap 03

Beekeeping Challenges

Beekeepers currently face **reactive** systems that fail to provide **proactive** decision support. There is a critical need for **real-time hornet detection** and **predictive honey harvesting** forecasts.

Missing: Real-time Action
Research Gap 04

Limited System Integration

Most smart beekeeping systems are fragmented, with limited integration between sensors, decision support, and **cloud analytics**. There is a need for a **unified platform** that combines these components.

Missing: Platform Integration
Research Gap 05

Real-Time Actionable Insights

While most systems provide data, the lack of **real-time actionable insights** for beekeepers limits their ability to take immediate actions to prevent colony loss or optimize honey production.

Missing: Actionable Insights
Research Gap 06

System Sustainability and Cost-Effectiveness

Although many systems exist, they often fail to address the **long-term sustainability** and **cost-effectiveness** for rural beekeepers. Most technologies require high **initial investment** and ongoing maintenance, which is not suitable for low-resource areas.

Missing: Cost-Effectiveness
